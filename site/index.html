Quickstart

Smaller downloads

Content-defined chunks are stored once across files and models, and a lossless float transform helps zstd on BF16 and F32 weights. Every chunk is checked against its hash.

Shared by peers

Machines on your LAN find each other on their own. Over the internet, peers find models on a DHT and connect through relays when behind NAT. Mount a model and start loading it before the download finishes.

Signed and auditable

Publishers sign what they publish, and the registry keeps an append-only, hash-chained log of every name and block. Nodes refuse blocked models and chunks.

How is it different?

vs Hugging Face

Why not download from the Hub?

Keep doing that: point HF_ENDPOINT at a chungus hub and your tools don't change. The first machine downloads from Hugging Face, and every other machine on your network gets it from that one.

vs BitTorrent

Isn't this just torrents?

A torrent is a fixed set of files, so a new version starts from zero. chungus shares chunks across files, models and versions, and fetches only the ones you're missing.

trust

How do I know it's the real model?

Publishers sign every model, and every name goes in a public, append-only log you can check with chungus audit. Each chunk is checked against its hash, and pickle files are refused.

speed

Do I wait for the whole thing?

On Linux, chungus mount shows the files at once and fetches chunks as they're read, so a model can start loading before the download finishes.
