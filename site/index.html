Quickstart

Smaller downloads

Content-defined chunks are stored once across files and models, and a lossless float transform helps zstd on BF16 and F32 weights. Every chunk is checked against its hash.

Shared by peers

Machines on your LAN find each other on their own. Over the internet, peers find models on a DHT and connect through relays when behind NAT. Mount a model and start loading it before the download finishes.

Signed and auditable

Publishers sign what they publish, and the registry keeps an append-only, hash-chained log of every name and block. Nodes refuse blocked models and chunks.
